1. Scope of Services

Provider will deliver cold email campaign setup and strategy services for Client, including:

  • Setup of sending infrastructure, including domains and inboxes
  • Configuration of cold email campaign systems
  • Initial campaign strategy and sequencing
  • Copywriting support for campaign messaging
  • Segmentation planning for target audiences
  • Planning for event-based outreach and attendee follow-up sequences

This agreement does not include landing page development or video production at this stage.

2. Fees & Payment Terms

Client agrees to pay Provider a total setup fee of $3,000, split into two payments: $1,500 due upfront via the checkout link on this page, and $1,500 due 30 days later. Work begins upon receipt of the first payment. Provider will send a checkout link for the second payment when it comes due.

This fee covers:

  • Domain and inbox setup
  • Annual purchase costs for domains and inboxes included in the setup period
  • Initial technical configuration and campaign buildout

The previously discussed $4,500 video content scope is not included in this agreement.

3. Software / Platform Costs

Campaign execution requires an email sending platform. Client agrees to cover the ongoing monthly platform cost required to accommodate the campaign's email volume, beginning at campaign launch. The exact amount will be confirmed with Client based on final sending volume before launch.

Any third-party software or tool costs not explicitly included above must be approved by Client before purchase.

4. Ongoing Optimization

Provider will provide ongoing optimization of the cold email campaign as part of the partnership and will not charge a separate maintenance fee at this time.

5. Lead Data

Provider will supply the initial batch of verified lead data, sufficient for approximately the first 30 days of sending. Thereafter, Client is responsible for providing lead lists on an ongoing basis, along with any associated data verification and enrichment costs.

6. Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to:

  • Participate in a kickoff and planning session
  • Provide timely feedback on campaign direction and messaging
  • Share relevant business information needed for targeting and segmentation
  • Provide access to event calendars, attendee data, and related materials if Client wants attendee follow-up campaigns included
  • Provide ongoing lead lists after the initial 30-day batch, including any verification and enrichment costs

7. Strategy and Deliverables

Initial planning may include:

  • Recommended number of domains
  • Domain warm-up timeline
  • Sequence structure
  • Audience segmentation
  • Integration of upcoming events into outreach strategy
  • Follow-up campaigns for existing event attendees

8. Email Compliance

Provider follows industry best practices for cold outreach, including opt-out handling and sending hygiene. However, Client is responsible for the legal review of outbound email compliance in the jurisdictions where Client operates and targets recipients, including but not limited to CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and CASL. Provider does not provide legal advice.

9. No Guarantee of Results

Provider will use reasonable professional efforts to execute and optimize campaigns. However, Provider does not guarantee any specific number of leads, meetings, conversions, or revenue outcomes.

10. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the initial setup and agreed launch work is completed, unless terminated earlier by either party in writing.

If terminated after work has begun, Client will remain responsible for payment for work completed and any approved third-party costs already incurred.

11. Intellectual Property

Client will own Client-specific campaign assets and business materials created under this agreement upon full payment, excluding Provider's pre-existing processes, frameworks, templates, and know-how.

12. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any non-public business, technical, or commercial information shared during the engagement.

13. Independent Contractor

Provider is acting as an independent contractor and not as an employee, partner, or legal representative of Client.

14. Acceptance & Electronic Execution

This Agreement is executed electronically. By entering their name and title below, checking the acceptance box, and completing payment through the checkout link on this page, Client's authorized representative agrees to be bound by all terms of this Agreement. Electronic acceptance carries the same legal effect as a handwritten signature.
