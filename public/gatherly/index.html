The Gap

Gatherly's Value Isn't
Showing Up Where It Counts

The platform is built. The experience is unlike anything else. But without marketing assets that capture it, the right communities have no way to find it.

Strong Product Videos. No Targeted Story.

Gatherly has videos that showcase the platform well. What's missing is content built specifically for community leaders: targeted assets that speak to their pain points, frame the value proposition for their world, and give them a clear reason to switch.

The Right Audience Isn't Looking

The ideal Gatherly customer is already paying for Zoom, frustrated but comfortable. Without proactive outreach, Gatherly is invisible to the communities that need it most and waiting for them to discover it on their own.

Interest Has Nowhere to Go

Even a warm prospect has no clear next step. Awareness without a connected funnel does not convert. The gap between "I've heard of Gatherly" and "I'm a paying licensee" is where growth stops.
