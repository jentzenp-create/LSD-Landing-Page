1 Scope of Services

Provider will run a monthly cold email outreach campaign promoting Client's Immersive HQ product, including:

  • Targeting two segments: Skool groups and independent coaching communities
  • Sourcing and verifying contact lists for those segments
  • Writing and testing email sequences, with demo bookings as the primary call to action
  • Sending campaigns on the cold email infrastructure Provider previously set up for Client
  • Responding to positive replies and routing them to Client's demo booking link
  • A short monthly report covering sends, replies and demos booked, plus ongoing optimization

2 Not Included

Branded roundtable hosting, affiliate partner recruiting and onboarding, sponsorships, landing page development and video production are not part of this agreement. Roundtables may be added in a separate agreement, tentatively in January.

3 Fees & Payment Terms

Client agrees to pay Provider $500 per month, billed as a recurring subscription through the checkout link on this page. The first payment is due on acceptance, and each following payment is charged automatically on the same date each month. Work begins upon receipt of the first payment.

4 Software / Platform Costs

The email sending platform cost continues to be covered by Client, as set out in the original setup agreement. Any new third-party software or tool costs must be approved by Client before purchase.

5 Client Responsibilities

Client agrees to:

  • Provide a demo booking link and confirm who on Client's team takes the demos
  • Share Immersive HQ pricing, positioning and any launch materials needed for messaging
  • Give timely feedback on sequences before they go out

Provider follows industry best practices for cold outreach, including opt-out handling and sending hygiene. However, Client is responsible for the legal review of outbound email compliance in the jurisdictions where Client operates and targets recipients, including but not limited to CAN-SPAM, GDPR, and CASL. Provider does not provide legal advice.

7 No Guarantee of Results

Provider will use reasonable professional efforts to run and optimize the campaign. However, Provider does not guarantee any specific number of replies, demos, subscriptions, or revenue outcomes.

8 Term and Cancellation

This Agreement runs month to month starting on the Effective Date. Either party may cancel in writing at any time, effective at the end of the current paid month. No further charges are made after cancellation, and fees already paid are non-refundable.

9 Intellectual Property

Client will own Client-specific campaign copy and lead lists created under this agreement for months that have been paid, excluding Provider's pre-existing processes, frameworks, templates, and know-how.

10 Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep confidential any non-public business, technical, or commercial information shared during the engagement.

11 Independent Contractor

Provider is acting as an independent contractor and not as an employee, partner, or legal representative of Client.
