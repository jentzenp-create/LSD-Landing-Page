Scope of Work

Six Workstreams, One Goal

Every piece of this sprint works toward the same outcome: more visibility, more bookings, and more repeat customers.

1
SEO Foundation & Website Optimization
  • Review current website performance, rankings, and search visibility
  • Establish a baseline for traffic, keyword visibility, and SEO strength
  • Identify priority search opportunities based on competitor positioning and customer demand
  • Optimize key website pages and on-site content for relevant search terms
  • Strengthen website SEO structure to support improved long-term visibility
  • Monitor performance and adjust priorities throughout the sprint
2
Homepage Conversion Refresh
  • Review the current homepage for clarity, trust, and conversion barriers
  • Make light design and copy updates to improve messaging and user flow
  • Strengthen calls to action and overall homepage structure
  • Improve how services, value, and booking information are presented
  • Complete updates within the existing website framework

This includes a light homepage refresh only — not a full website redesign or rebuild.

3
Weekly Email Newsletter
  • Create and send one email newsletter per week
  • Re-engage past customers and stay top of mind with existing contacts
  • Write content aligned with the brand voice and values
  • Use newsletter communication to support relationship-based marketing and repeat business
4
Review & Follow-Up Automation
  • Set up or improve post-booking follow-up communication
  • Create automated review request messaging
  • Support an increase in customer reviews over time
  • Build a simple communication process that encourages referrals and repeat bookings
5
Customer Re-Engagement Emails
  • Develop and send light marketing or follow-up emails to previous customers
  • Encourage repeat bookings through simple, timely outreach
  • Improve ongoing communication without requiring constant manual follow-up
6
Reporting & Strategy Oversight
  • Take an initial performance snapshot at the start of the engagement
  • Provide monthly progress reports
  • Track movement in traffic, search visibility, engagement, and review activity
  • Adjust strategy based on what is working best during the sprint
