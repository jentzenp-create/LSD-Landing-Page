How a conversation runs

Confidential from the first call until the board is ready to announce.

  1. A private first call

    You speak with the partner who leads the seat, not a coordinator. Nothing is shared beyond that partner without your say.

  2. Discovery

    An intensive look at the business, the board and what the role truly needs before anyone is approached.

  3. A selection roadmap

    A plan built for this seat: who to consider, how they are assessed, and when the board decides.

  4. A decision that lasts

    We stay close through the appointment and the first months, so the outcome holds.
