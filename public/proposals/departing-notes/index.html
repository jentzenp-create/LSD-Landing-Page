Inbox Setup & Warm-Up

Built in week one. Sending by month two.

Cold email only works when it lands in the inbox, not spam. So before a single attorney hears from us, we build a dedicated sending setup and warm it up for 21 days. Your main departingnotes domain is never put at risk.

  • Week 1: domains & inboxes. We register 5 lookalike sending domains and set up 10 professional inboxes (2 per domain), each with a real name and signature.
  • Week 1: authentication. SPF, DKIM and DMARC records configured on every domain, plus forwarding back to your main site so every link looks legitimate.
  • Days 1 to 21: warm-up. Each inbox exchanges real conversations with a warm-up network, starting at a handful a day and ramping up, so email providers learn to trust it.
  • Meanwhile: list & copy. While inboxes warm, we build and verify the attorney list and write the outreach sequence for your approval.
  • Month 2: outreach live. Sending ramps to about 30 emails per inbox per day, with warm-up kept running in the background to protect deliverability.
  • All costs covered. Domains, inboxes, warm-up and sending software, and attorney data are included in the $1,000 retainer.
