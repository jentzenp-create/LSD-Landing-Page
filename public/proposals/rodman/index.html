The Cost of Waiting

What Doesn't Get Built
Stays Locked to the Room

This isn't a "sale ends Friday" argument — it's a logical one. Three things are true today that won't necessarily stay true indefinitely.

Every Month It Stays a Live Talk

Reach stays capped by Richard's calendar. The material keeps working exactly as well as it always has — for the room it's delivered to, and no one else. That ceiling doesn't lower itself.

Joe's Warm Network Is Open Now

The contacts and firm relationships that make Phase 1 deployable at zero cold-start cost are available today. Relationships and timing shift — the easiest version of this pilot is the one built while that access is warm.

The Source Is Easiest to Capture Directly

The most valuable input to this whole system is Richard, on camera, explaining this in his own words. That's the one part of Phase 1 nothing can substitute for later — everything downstream depends on capturing it well, now.

None of this is about pressure — it's about sequencing. The $30,000 doesn't buy urgency, it buys the difference between "this reach stays possible" and "this reach stays theoretical." The pilot is what turns the vision from something we discuss into something we can point to.
