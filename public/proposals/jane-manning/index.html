What I heard on our call

You have the tree. Now let’s get the fruit off it.

  • The message is already there

    Your paintings say something people feel right away: our bodies are an internal universe, and we’re lucky to live in them. When people meet you, they get hooked. They just have nowhere to go next.

  • The tees are the first door

    Organic, limited runs, art you can wear for $45 to $55. Not everyone buys a painting, but plenty of people will buy the shirt and tell the story every time they wear it. You have stock, some of it at the spa in Sebastopol, and new paintings ready to print.

  • The business side drains you

    How many shirts are left, what shipped, when to reorder, which painting becomes the next shirt. You’d rather paint, and you should. That part can be handled for you.

Fig. 3 · Circulation

Your job isn’t to sell. It’s to be heard.

$500per month · after launch

You’re at your best in conversation. So the monthly work is about putting you in more rooms, and keeping the shop and agent sharp so every new fan has somewhere to go.

  • DistributionPitching you as a guest on podcasts in our network, and finding speaking slots where your message fits.
  • CommunityAn introduction to the Acres of Diamonds Roundtable, a relationship first group that opens doors to bigger audiences.
  • CareHosting, updates, new products and paintings added, and anything that breaks gets fixed.
  • OptimizeWatching what sells and where people drop off, then improving the shop and the agent every month.
  • Check inA monthly call to look at the numbers in plain language and plan the next drop.
