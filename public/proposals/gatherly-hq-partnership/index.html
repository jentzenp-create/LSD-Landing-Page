Where Things Stand

Onboarding Has to Scale Carefully.

Immersive HQ is pre-revenue, onboarding opens at 10 teams a week, and the last sponsorship model left some scar tissue. The next phase needs a partner who brings qualified demand without recreating the same accountability problems.

Onboarding Capacity Is Limited

Dirk and Micah are building the low-touch onboarding flow. At 10 teams a week to start, HQ needs a steady stream of the right operators, not a flood of signups it can't support yet.

The Old Sponsorship Model Created Friction

When sponsors funded communities that Gatherly deployed and managed, complaints and refunds landed on your team instead of the sponsor. Unicorn Universe was the clearest example.

Cash Is Tighter Post-Refunds

HQ is pre-revenue and the team is rightly careful about new fixed costs. Any new engagement has to carry its own weight and prove itself quickly.
