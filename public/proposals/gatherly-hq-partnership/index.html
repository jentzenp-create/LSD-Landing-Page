Where Things Stand

HQ Launches Nov 13.
Onboarding Has to Scale Carefully.

Immersive HQ is pre-revenue, capacity opens at just 10 teams a week, and the last sponsorship model left scar tissue. The next phase of growth needs a partner who brings qualified demand without creating the same accountability problems.

Low-Touch Onboarding, Not Yet at Volume

Dirk and Micah are building the self-serve onboarding flow, but at 10 teams a week to start, HQ needs a steady, qualified stream of the right operators landing in that funnel — not a flood of unqualified signups it can't yet support.

The Old Sponsorship Model Created Friction

When sponsors funded communities that Gatherly deployed and managed directly, complaints and refunds landed on your team instead of the sponsor. Unicorn Universe was the clearest example of that mismatch.

Cash Is Tighter Post-Refunds

HQ is pre-revenue and the team is understandably cautious about new fixed costs. Any new engagement needs to carry its own weight and prove itself quickly, not just add overhead.
