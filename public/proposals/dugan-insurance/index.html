2
Video Content, YouTube-First

The single biggest gap today is that there's no video or social presence at all. Short, local-focused videos ("How Medicare Works in Bakersfield," client testimonials, plan-comparison explainers) get posted to YouTube — a real search engine in its own right for this kind of query — then cut down into GBP posts, Facebook, and Instagram content from a single shoot.

i
Where AI avatars fit, and where they don't. Tools like HeyGen are a good fit for lower-stakes, purely educational pieces (FAQ-style explainers) where a script-to-video workflow saves real production time. Testimonials and anything trust-critical should stay as real footage of Sam — an AI avatar delivering a client testimonial reads as inauthentic to this audience, which defeats the purpose. Any AI-generated content should also be cleared with Sam's FMO/carrier compliance team before use.
3
Compliant Review & Referral Engine

Reviews are the top local-pack ranking factor and the cheapest lever available — this is a workflow build inside the CRM Sam already pays for (automated post-appointment review requests), not new spend.

Automated Review Requests

Built inside the CRM Sam already has — post-appointment review requests go out automatically, no new tooling or spend required.

Referral Program, Built to CMS Rules

No cash or cash-equivalent rewards. Nominal gifts capped at $15 per referral and $75 per client per year. Gift cards to local businesses only, never big-box retailers. The gift is given regardless of whether the referred person enrolls, and offered equally to every client who refers someone.

!
This program should be reviewed and approved by Sam's FMO or carrier compliance contact before launch, since referral programs are typically subject to plan-year sign-off.
