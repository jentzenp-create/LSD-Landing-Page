The Situation

A Lot of Calls,
Not a Lot of Hands

A radio ad airs Wednesday, September 24, alongside a fair banner and several thousand mailers — all driving calls to a second number. At the same time, Sam's team of 5 agents will be stretched thin working the fair in person, with several tied up around an upcoming congregation privilege. Without something answering that line, overflow calls become lost leads.

Hard Deadline

Radio ad airs September 24. The second line needs to be live and tested before then, not built around it.

Team Stretched Thin

5 agents working the fair in person and a congregation privilege overlapping the same window — fewer hands to pick up the phone.

The Fix, in Two Parts

Automate the intake Sam already built (the JotForm), then put Linda on the second number to catch what the team can't answer live.

Phase 1 · Starts Immediately
1
Lead Intake Automation on the Existing Line

Sam has already built a bilingual (English/Spanish) JotForm intake that collects everything needed to start a Medicare application — primary care doctor, specialist, phone, email, and more. Right now, when a caller expresses interest, Linda takes that information down manually. This phase teaches her to hand it off to the form instead.

Intent Detection & Text Handoff

When a caller says something like "I'd like to enroll" or asks about a Medicare consultation, Linda offers to text the intake form link on the spot — in English or Spanish, matching the caller.

Two-Stage Lead Notifications

Sam and any team members he chooses are notified when a caller speaks with the AI but doesn't complete the form, and again when a caller does complete it. Notifications can go out by text, email, or both — per person — so nothing sits unseen.

Manual Lead Distribution, Sam in Control

Completed leads are emailed to Sam with full details so he can review and forward opportunities to Madison, Gabrielle, Dominic, or Griselda himself. Automated round-robin or weighted distribution can be layered on later if he wants it.

Phase 2 · Live Before Sept 24
2
Second Voice AI Agent for the Open Enrollment Push

The radio ad, fair banner, and mailers all drive to a second number. Rather than let it ring to voicemail while the team works the fair, Linda answers it directly — engaging the caller and routing interested ones straight to the same intake form built in Phase 1.

In Scope

Voice AI answers the second number, converses naturally with the caller, and texts the intake form to anyone interested — same form, same follow-up flow as the main line.

Explicitly Out of Scope (For Now)

No scheduling or booking functionality on this line yet — that's a "maybe later." Sam will personally handle distributing leads and submissions evenly among the agents, same as Phase 1.

Phase 3 · Starts After Sept 24, ~30 Days
3
Website Migration & Redesign

Once the pre-event automation is live and stable, focus shifts to needmedicareplan.com itself: moving it off its current page-builder platform and onto a modern foundation that supports fast, ongoing design and content changes — then rebuilding the design fully around Sam's new logo so the site, Google Business Profile, and social presence all look uniform.

Platform Migration

Move the site to a faster, more flexible platform for making design and content changes — new pages, new offers, new copy — without being stuck in a slow page builder.

Full Design Refresh

Rebuilt around the new logo, with the compliance and trust fixes already flagged (CMS disclaimer, HTTPS, review widget, consistent experience claims) addressed as part of the rebuild.

✓
GoHighLevel doesn't go anywhere. Calendar, CRM, and Linda all stay exactly as they are — the new site connects to them through an embed, so booking and lead flow keep working the same way for Sam and the team. Only the public-facing website itself moves.
