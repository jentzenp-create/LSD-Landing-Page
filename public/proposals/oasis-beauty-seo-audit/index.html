Critical Finding

The backlink profile isn't neutral — it's actively harmful.

This is the single most important thing to show the client. Someone built (or bought) a bulk, low-quality link network pointing at this domain — and it's routed through gambling sites in another market entirely.

492 of 929 referring domains sit at Authority Score 2 — the bottom of Semrush's scale. More than half the entire link profile is concentrated at the lowest possible quality tier. That's not a natural distribution; it's the signature of a bulk link-farm purchase.
⚠ Spam / PBN link network detected
  • 7.78 million backlinks from only 929 referring domains — an average of ~8,300 links per domain. Real link-building never looks like this; link farms do.
  • 492 of those 929 domains sit at Authority Score 2, the very bottom of Semrush's scale — more than half the link profile is near-worthless by design.
  • The top linking domains have zero topical relevance to a med spa: recipesown.com, getmomapp.com, cuecca.com.ar, funlink.site, muruganframe.in, hoodgandul.site — scattered across a dozen countries.

The anchor text is the smoking gun — these are the exact words other sites use to link to oasisbeauty.life:

mislibet mislibet giriş bets10 casibom holiganbet jojobet

Those are Turkish online-gambling brand names. And some anchors describe exactly what was purchased, in plain text:

"high quality dofollow backlinks DA50 PA40 premium PBN network service oasisbeauty.life rank first page google fast seo link building buy backlinks online cheap"
"reliable SEO authority backlinks for oasisbeauty.life to boost domain authority... grow search traffic, with safe link velocity."

Verdict: this reads like a cheap, bulk PBN package bought from an overseas link vendor — not SEO work. For a medical practice, a profile like this isn't just ineffective, it's a standing risk of a Google manual action. This needs to be disavowed and cleaned before any new link building starts.
