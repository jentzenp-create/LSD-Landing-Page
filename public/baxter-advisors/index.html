The Gap

The Pipeline Isn't Built Yet.

Baxter Advisors has the operational track record to run a company at the executive level. But a fractional practice only grows as fast as the visibility and outbound behind it.

Deep Experience. No Authority Content.

Years of running operations at the executive level don't automatically translate into content that buyers find when they search for help. Without a website and a story built specifically for the fractional COO pitch, that track record stays invisible.

The Right Buyers Aren't Hearing From You

Your ideal clients are specific enough to target directly:

  • PE/VC firms with fintechs, insurance rollups, or RIA aggregators in portfolio
  • Founder-led fintechs and insurance brokerages
  • 50-500 people, $20M-$500M revenue

Without proactive outreach, those buyers never hear the pitch.

A Referral Isn't a System

Word of mouth can fill one or two seats, but it isn't reliable enough to keep three to four client slots full at all times. Without a repeatable pipeline, revenue swings with whoever happens to refer next.

The Cost of Waiting

The Real Question Isn't
What This Costs. It's What Delay Costs.

Industry data puts full-time COO compensation for operators with your background around $500,000 a year. Baxter Advisors is built to match or beat that benchmark across three to four fractional clients. Every month without a system to fill those seats is a month of that target going unmet.

The math behind the target

Annual revenue target: $500,000 — the industry benchmark for full-time COO compensation at your level of experience.
At a full roster of 3 clients, that's roughly $166,700/client/yr (~$13,900/mo per client).
At a full roster of 4 clients, that's roughly $125,000/client/yr (~$10,400/mo per client).
Spread across a year, the target run-rate is ~$41,700 per month — that's the value of every month Baxter Advisors operates at full capacity, and the value of every month it doesn't.
