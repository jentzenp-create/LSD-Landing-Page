# Virtual Exhibitor Booth Talk Tracks

## 1. Opening (30 seconds)

"Hey everyone, thanks for stopping by the booth. I'm Jentzen with Local Sun Digital. I'm not going to pitch you on anything right away. Instead, I want to show you something on the page in front of you and get a real-time read on this room. You should all see a page loaded up already. Let's start with a quick poll."

---

## 2. Poll Activation (1-2 minutes)

"You'll see a question: *Where are you on your AI journey?* Four options. Go ahead and pick the one that fits you best. Don't overthink it. And watch what happens when you vote."

*Wait for votes to start rolling in. The live animations should be firing.*

"Look at that. You can see the votes coming in live. The bars are filling up. [React to what you see] Looks like we've got a lot of people in the Experimenting category. That's actually really common. Most business owners have played with ChatGPT but haven't figured out how to make it do real work yet."

**If mostly Curious/Experimenting:**

"That's perfect, honestly. You're in the right place. What I'm going to show you next is going to make this way more concrete."

**If mostly Implementing/Systematized:**

"We've got some power users in the room. Love it. What I'm about to show you might confirm what you already know, or it might reveal a gap you haven't thought about."

---

## 3. ROI Calculator Walkthrough (2-3 minutes)

"Scroll down past the poll. You'll see a tool called 'What is manual work actually costing you?' This is free, and I want you to actually use it right now. Think about your week. How many hours do you spend on things like follow-up emails, scheduling, content, onboarding clients, invoicing. Plug in real numbers. Be honest with yourself."

*Give people 30-45 seconds to fill it in.*

"Now look at your results. That number at the bottom, the annual opportunity. That's what you're leaving on the table every year doing work that an AI agent could handle for you. And I don't mean 'someday.' I mean with tools that exist right now."

"And if you hover over those lifestyle cards at the bottom, you'll see what you could do with that time instead. [Pause] Yeah, those are videos. Touch grass. Play with your dog. Take Fridays off. That's not hypothetical. That's what recovering even five or ten hours a week actually looks like."

---

## 4. Workshop Pitch (1 minute)

"So here's the thing. Knowing the number is step one. Knowing what to do about it is step two. That's why I'm running a free workshop next Tuesday, June 17 at 11am Pacific. It's called *AI Agents That Pay For Themselves.*"

"It's not a sales pitch. It's a working session. I'm going to walk you through exactly which tasks to automate first, which tools to use, and how to get started without needing a developer or a tech team. You'll leave with a plan you can act on that same week."

"If that sounds useful, scroll down to the workshop section and put your email in. It takes five seconds. You'll get registered on Luma and I'll send you the details before Tuesday."

---

## 5. Audit Pitch (1 minute)

"Now, some of you looked at that ROI number and thought, 'Okay, but what does this actually look like for MY business specifically?' That's what the AI Audit is for."

"It's a one-on-one session with me. One hour. $97. I look at your actual business, your workflows, your tools, and I map out exactly where AI fits. You walk away with a custom plan, not a generic checklist. Specific tools, specific workflows, specific next steps."

"If you saw a big number in that calculator, this is how you go from knowing the problem to solving it. There are only a few slots available this week, so if you're interested, scroll down and grab one."

---

## 6. Soft Close (30 seconds)

"And if you're not ready for the workshop or the audit yet, totally fine. At the very bottom of the page there's a spot to grab our free AI Quick-Start Checklist. Just drop your email and we'll send it over. No strings."

"Thanks for your time, everyone. I'll stick around if anyone has questions."

---

## Handling Q&A

**"What kind of tasks can AI actually handle?"**

"The big ones are lead follow-up, scheduling, content repurposing, client onboarding, and admin like invoicing. Basically anything repetitive where you're doing the same steps over and over. That's what agents are built for."

**"Is this just ChatGPT?"**

"ChatGPT is one piece. What we're talking about are AI agents, systems that run on their own. They don't wait for you to type a prompt. They watch for a trigger, like a new lead coming in, and they handle the whole workflow automatically."

**"Why is the audit only $97?"**

"Because I'd rather give you something genuinely useful at a price that's a no-brainer than try to sell you a $5,000 package you're not ready for. If the audit reveals a real opportunity and you want help implementing it, we can talk about that. But the audit stands on its own."
